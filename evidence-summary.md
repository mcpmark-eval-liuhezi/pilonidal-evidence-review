# Pilonidal Sinus Disease — Surgical Treatment Evidence Summary

Journal club evidence pack: studies of surgical treatment outcomes in pilonidal sinus disease, focused on **long-term recurrence** and **postoperative infection**.

Five most relevant studies from PubMed, with full publication details:

---

## 1. PMID 39174833
- **Title:** Long-term follow-up of pilonidal sinus disease treated by radial laser surgery.
- **Journal:** Langenbeck's archives of surgery, 2024
- **Authors:** Karita, Adalia, Tuija, Jukka, Kethe
- **Takeaway:** Retrospective series reporting long-term follow-up (healing rates and recurrences) of radial laser surgery for PSD — a minimally invasive technique where prior evidence was limited to small samples and short follow-up.

## 2. PMID 38725367
- **Title:** A new midline closure technique without skin sutures: Long-term outcomes of primary repair of pilonidal sinus disease.
- **Journal:** Anales del sistema sanitario de Navarra, 2024
- **Authors:** Tas, Karahan
- **Takeaway:** Comparative study of two surgical techniques (a sutureless new midline closure vs. conventional closure), measuring postoperative long-term complications and recurrence after primary repair of PSD.

## 3. PMID 37837259
- **Title:** Postoperative results of modified Karydakis flap method for primary sacrococcygeal pilonidal sinus disease: short and long-term analysis.
- **Journal:** ANZ journal of surgery, 2024
- **Authors:** Erdoğrul
- **Takeaway:** Single-method outcome analysis of the modified Karydakis flap in patients with primary sacrococcygeal pilonidal sinus, reporting both short- and long-term postoperative results including complications and recurrence.

## 4. PMID 35779104
- **Title:** Long-term results of EPSiT in children and adolescents: still the right way to go.
- **Journal:** Pediatric surgery international, 2022
- **Authors:** Erculiani, Mottadelli, Carlini, Barbetta, Dusio, Pini Prato
- **Takeaway:** Unicentric series of pediatric and adolescent patients who underwent endoscopic pilonidal sinus treatment (EPSiT) over a 5-year period, reporting long-term outcomes including complications and recurrence.

## 5. PMID 34021885
- **Title:** Pediatric endoscopic pilonidal sinus treatment (PEPSiT): what we learned after a 3-year experience in the pediatric population.
- **Journal:** Updates in surgery, 2021
- **Authors:** Esposito, Montaruli, Autorino, Mendoza-Sagaon, Escolino
- **Takeaway:** Multi-institutional retrospective review of 152 pediatric patients (<18 years, primary or recurrent PSD) treated with PEPSiT (2017–2020), reporting healing rates, postoperative complications including wound infections (8/152), and disease recurrence (7/152, 4.6%).
